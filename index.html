    About

    Mission & Vision

    Gayatri Pariwar is a living model of a futuristic society guided by human unity and equality: a modern adaptation of the wisdom of the Vedic Rishis and Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam, the whole world is one family. Founded by Yug Rishi Vedmurti Taponishth Pandit Shriram Sharma Acharya Ji, it has become a mass movement for the Transformation of the Era.

    Aims & Objectives

    Rise of divinity in humankind, descent of heaven on earth.

    Individual development, family development and social upliftment.

    Healthy body, pure mind and civilised society.

    “Atmavat sarvabhooteshu” and “Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam”.

    One Nation, One Language (love), One Religion (humanity), One Government (self-governance).

    Equal opportunity for self-growth irrespective of caste, colour or creed.

    Origin of Mission

    After 24 years of Gayatri Sadhana, Gurudev founded Gayatri Tapobhumi, Mathura in 1953. The grand Gayatri Yajna of 1958 launched the Yug Nirman Yojna.

    History & Achievements

    From one sadhak's practice to a mass movement: 5,000+ active centres, branches in 80+ countries, and a university founded in 2002.

    Social Reforms

    Campaigns against blind faith, fundamentalism, dowry, expensive weddings and funerals, and addictions.

    Solemn Pledge (Satsankalpa)

    The manifesto of the movement, to be read every morning.

    Vichar Kranti Abhiyan

    The movement for the refinement of thinking.

    Philosophy

    Three Pillars of Strength

    • Will & patronage of the Almighty
    • Spiritual guidance & protection of Rishis
    • Effort & participation of enlightened masses

    Two rules of self-refinement

    • Noble deeds flow from noble thoughts
    • Simple living, high thinking

    Patron Founder · A Visionary of Bright Future

    Vedmurti Taponishtha Pt. Shriram Sharma Acharya

    Born 20 September 1911 in Anwalkheda, Agra district, U.P. He completed 24 Mahapurascharanas of the Gayatri Mahamantra, served as a freedom-fighter volunteer and was jailed several times, and worked for social and moral upliftment with Mahatma Gandhi's blessings.

    He created the 100-point Yug Nirman Yojna, translated the entire Vedic literature into Hindi and wrote 2,400+ (about 3,000) books. He founded Shantikunj, Brahmavarchas Research Institute, Akhand Jyoti Sansthan, Gayatri Tapobhumi (Mathura) and thousands of Shaktipeeths.

    He left his body on Gayatri Jayanti, 2 June 1990. 41 Ashwamedha Yagyas followed: 32 in India and 9 abroad, including the UK, USA, Canada, Australia and South Africa.

    24Mahapurascharanas
    2,400+Books
    41Ashwamedha Yagyas

    Vandaniya Mata Ji

    Mata Bhagwati Devi Sharma

    Took over the mission in 1990. The Shraddhanjali Samaroh of October 1990 drew 15,00,000+ devotees. In 1992 she announced the Ashwamedha Yagya series, starting in Jaipur in November 1992, and in 1995 led the Pratham Poornahuti Samaroh at Anwalkheda.

    Present Mentor

    Shraddheya Dr. Pranav Pandya Ji

    • Head, All World Gayatri Pariwar
    • Chancellor, Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya
    • Director, Brahmavarchas Research Institute
    • Editor, Akhand Jyoti
    • President, Swami Vivekananda Yogvidya Mahapeetham

    Born on Roop Chaturdashi, 1950, a day celebrated as Chetna Diwas. A gold-medalist MD, he qualified for US medical services in 1976 but stayed in India at his Guru's call.

    A pioneer of scientific spirituality, he established Gayatri Pariwar branches in 80+ countries, and under his leadership Dev Sanskriti University was founded in 2002.

    Our pioneering youthful representative

    Dr. Chinmay Pandya

    • Grandson of Pt. Shriram Sharma Acharya
    • Pro Vice Chancellor, Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya
    • Editor, “Dev Sanskriti” interdisciplinary international journal
    • Vice President, Indian Yoga Association

    After his medical education in India he earned the MRCPsych (UK) and served as Associate Specialist in Older People Services at West London Mental Health Trust, working in dementia and Alzheimer's care. He returned to India and DSVV in 2010.

    Gayatri

    ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात्।

    उस प्राणस्वरूप, दुःखनाशक, सुखस्वरूप, श्रेष्ठ, तेजस्वी, पापनाशक, देवस्वरूप परमात्मा को हम अन्तःकरण में धारण करें। वह परमात्मा हमारी बुद्धि को सन्मार्ग में प्रेरित करे।

    Gayatri Sadhana: the daily practice of the mantra at the centre of the movement.

    Yagya

    “Ayam Yajna Vishvasya Bhuvanasya Nabhih” (Atharva Veda 9.15.14): Yagya is the fundamental process of nature.

    Physically, it is the offering of herbs into the holy fire with the sound energy of mantras, with therapeutic and environmental benefits. Literally, it means selfless sacrifice for noble purposes.

    Thought Transformation

    The Vichar Kranti Abhiyan rests on one idea: “this entire world is a creation of thoughts”.

    • Dissemination of Righteous Knowledge
    • Mass Awakening Campaign
    • Advent of New Era
    Scientific Spirituality

    The integration of science and spirituality, with spirituality understood as the science of consciousness.

    Sacramental Rites

    The 16 sanskaras (Shodash Sanskar). Sanskars are performed daily at Shantikunj at 5 AM, with no prior appointment needed, except Vivah Sanskar: arrive a day before.

    Shared accommodation and food are provided free.

    Indian Culture
